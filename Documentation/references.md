## Hi-COLA applications and validation

- [K-mouflage applications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.00855): model-specific use of Hi-COLA beyond its native Vainshtein settings.
- [Comparison of approximate large-scale-structure methods](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13667): the comparison exercise linked by the project README.

The team can be contacted at [team.hicola@gmail.com](mailto:team.hicola@gmail.com); individual researcher contact details are in the papers.
